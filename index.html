Mert Turna

I'm the Co-Founder of Derya (YC S26). We're bringing forth the fifth industrial revolution by hyperconnecting industrial companies with agents. It's my second YC company; before that I studied Computer Science at UCLA.

I was born and raised in Istanbul. I spent my teenage years on the Turkish national debating team, which taught me that most arguments are really arguments about definitions, and that being right is a much smaller part of persuasion than I wanted it to be.

My other passion is continental philosophy. Mark Fisher's hauntology is the frame I can't shake: we live among the ghosts of futures that were promised and never arrived, and nowhere is that more literal than in heavy industry, where the machines are decades ahead of the paperwork used to buy and sell them. I'm also interested in Deleuze's societies of control and what they become when agents, rather than people, do most of the coordinating. Conveniently, the thing I build all day is also the best instrument I have for looking into both.

I'd love to talk about any of this. Reach me below.
