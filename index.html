Mert Turna

I'm the Co-Founder of Derya (YC S26). We're bringing forth the fifth industrial revolution by hyperconnecting industrial companies with agents. It's my second YC company; before that I studied Computer Science at UCLA.

I was born and raised in Istanbul. I debated from the age of thirteen, most of it on the Turkish national team. For a long time that was my main way of satisfying a curiosity about how the world actually works underneath, its plumbing and its blood vessels. Building a company is how I get to touch that at scale.

My other passion is continental philosophy. Mark Fisher's hauntology is the frame I can't shake: we live among the ghosts of futures that were promised and never arrived, and nowhere is that more literal than in heavy industry, where the machines are decades ahead of the paperwork used to buy and sell them. I'm also interested in Deleuze's societies of control and what they become when agents, rather than people, do most of the coordinating.

That's also where my research interests sit: long-horizon agents acting on real-world state, where the hard problem is less intelligence than verification and trust, and what markets look like when both sides of a negotiation are models. The product I build all day turns out to be a good instrument for studying both.

I'd love to talk about any of this. Reach me below.
